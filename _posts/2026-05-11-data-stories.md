As I have ascended into the corporate world where I am working with a vast amount of data that doesn't spark as much joy as data pulled from wikimedia, I am constantly engaging for a variety of weird and unique data stories that don't just drive and emphasize stakeholder value or business use cases. 

1. Data & Eggs - This is run by theDataFace (a staple in the data community)
2. Information is Beautiful - A staple in the information community 
3. Data is Plural - I first discovered this during my time at LOC!! 
4. data.gov - this one is basic but also a goodie. open data is cool!!! especially from our weird govts (local, state and federal)
5. Perthirtysix - The BEST data stories around truly. 
6. storytelling with data

json - you can just write one up seriously
